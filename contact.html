Common questions

Quick answers before you write to us.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Exhibit Binder Studio runs in your browser. There's no desktop plugin to install or keep updated.

Where are my documents stored?

In US-only storage, separated from every other organization's data at the database level.

What can I export?

A single combined, bookmarked PDF of the full binder, or a ZIP scoped to one section.

Which conventions does it follow?

It was built around US exhibit and Bates conventions from the start.

Does the QC check replace attorney review?

No. The checks are a fast structural first pass. Your team still reviews the binder against your court's specific rules.
