Why it matters

The part of binder prep that breaks under deadline.

Today, most exhibit binders are still assembled largely by hand. Every last-minute document swap means redoing the same three jobs.

In Acrobat

Renumbering pages

Every inserted or removed page shifts everything after it.

In Word

Retyping the exhibit index

The index is a separate document that falls out of date on the next change.

After every swap

Rebuilding bookmarks

Bookmarks point to the old pages until someone fixes them one by one.

Exhibit Binder Studio's core promise: pagination, exhibit numbering, Bates numbers and bookmarks stay synchronized automatically, no matter how many times the binder is edited.
