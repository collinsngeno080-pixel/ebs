Court-specific QC

Quality checks built for the way courts actually file.

Seven presets run a fast structural pass before anything leaves your desk.

  • US Federal: Generic Structural Checks
  • US Federal: Technical Filing (Fed. R. Civ. P. 5.2)
  • S.D.N.Y.: Individual Practice Checks
  • N.D. Cal.: Standing Order Checks
  • Delaware Court of Chancery: Format Checks
  • State Court: Generic Checks
  • PII & Sensitive Data Scan
  • More presets on the roadmap

These are structural and pattern-based checks. They verify your binder against consistent formatting and data-handling rules, then surface anything that needs a human's eyes. They're a fast first pass, not a substitute for attorney review of your specific court's rules.
